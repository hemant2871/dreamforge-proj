About DreamForge
A student-led game dev collective
DreamForge is a focused community of game developers who believe in learning by shipping. Core team currently led by B.Tech CSE students.

What this community is about

Not just a normal WhatsApp group – yaha pe log milke real games banate hain.

  • Team-based development of small but complete mobile games.
  • Discussions on game design, art, code and monetization.
  • Playtesting sessions, feedback loops and polishing sprints.
  • Help for publishing on Play Store & improving quality.
  • Beginners ke liye safe & supportive space.

Inside the DreamForge toolbox
We keep the stack simple and student-friendly, so new developers can join in quickly and still ship polished mobile games.

Unity (C#)

Godot
